# Nzuko AI personal-data incident response

1. Contain the incident: revoke exposed credentials, isolate affected services and preserve relevant logs.
2. Record discovery time, reporter, systems, people, data categories, approximate volumes and actions taken.
3. Assess confidentiality, integrity and availability impact and the likelihood/severity of harm to people.
4. Notify affected customer controllers without undue delay where Rohari acts as processor.
5. Where Rohari is controller, decide whether ICO notification is required and preserve the reasoning. Report notifiable breaches within 72 hours of awareness where feasible.
6. Notify affected individuals without undue delay where the breach is likely to create a high risk, using clear practical advice.
7. Recover securely, monitor for misuse and validate replacement credentials.
8. Complete a post-incident review and update the DPIA, controls and supplier records.

Never place secret values or unnecessary personal data in the incident ticket.
