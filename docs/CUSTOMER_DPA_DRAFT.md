# Nzuko AI customer data processing agreement — working draft

**Status: requires review and completion by a qualified UK data-protection professional before use.**

Parties: the customer identified in the order form (Controller) and RohariGroup LTD (Processor).

## Processing details

- Subject matter: authorised conversation ingestion, transcription, draft report generation, action tracking and related support.
- Duration: the subscription term plus documented deletion and backup cycles.
- Purpose: provide the Nzuko AI service on the Controller's documented instructions.
- Data subjects: authorised workspace users, conversation participants, workers, contractors, customers and other people mentioned in supplied content.
- Data: account identifiers, conversation content, voice-note transcripts, speaker labels, timestamps, reports, actions and audit metadata.
- Sensitive data: prohibited by default. Any approved special-category use requires written scope, an Article 9 condition, a DPIA and enhanced controls.

## Required terms to convert into final contractual language

1. Process personal data only on the Controller's documented instructions, including transfers.
2. Ensure authorised personnel are bound by confidentiality.
3. Maintain appropriate technical and organisational security measures.
4. Obtain general written authorisation for listed subprocessors, notify changes and allow objections.
5. Impose equivalent data-protection duties on every subprocessor; Rohari remains responsible for their performance.
6. Assist with access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability requests.
7. Assist with security, breach notifications, DPIAs and prior consultation.
8. At termination, return or delete personal data at the Controller's choice unless law requires retention.
9. Provide compliance information and permit proportionate audits.
10. Notify the Controller if an instruction appears to infringe applicable data-protection law.

## Security schedule

Include authentication, tenant separation, least privilege, encryption in transit, secret management, logging, backups, vulnerability management, incident response, retention enforcement, identifier minimisation, human approval and supplier review.

## Transfer schedule

List each processing country and applicable adequacy regulation or safeguard. Attach the UK IDTA or UK Addendum and transfer-risk assessment where required.
