# Nzuko AI Group Message

Here is a simple message you can post in the WhatsApp group:

Nzuko AI is a small assistant we are testing for our WhatsApp group. Its job is to help us capture decisions, action items, unresolved issues, and key points from both chats and voice notes so that discussions do not keep repeating. The admin will review any summary before it is shared back to the group. It is only for this approved group, not for private chats, and it is being used to help us keep clearer records of what has already been discussed.
