Nzuko AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Nzuko AI helps approved communities and teams turn authorised WhatsApp, Telegram and imported conversations into reports, action items and voice-note summaries. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

What we collect

  • Account data such as your email address, display name, and authentication details.
  • Workspace settings such as approved WhatsApp or Telegram group names, language settings, and report preferences.
  • Captured group content that your workspace explicitly approves for recap generation, including text messages and voice-note transcripts.
  • Billing and subscription data required to manage paid access through Stripe.
  • Operational logs used to secure the app, diagnose issues, and verify workspace activity.

How we use data

  • To authenticate users and protect access to each workspace.
  • To generate meeting minutes, recap drafts, and voice-note summaries.
  • To support billing, subscription changes, and customer support.
  • To improve reliability, detect misuse, and audit important workspace actions.

Third-party services

Nzuko AI currently relies on third-party infrastructure providers, which may process limited data on our behalf, including:

  • Supabase for authentication.
  • Stripe for payments and subscription management.
  • Netlify for hosting and backend functions.
  • OpenAI for voice-note transcription and AI-generated recap processing when enabled.
  • WAHA or related WhatsApp bridge services for approved WhatsApp connectivity.
  • Telegram's MTProto service through an isolated connector for QR-authorised Telegram connectivity.

Data retention

Nzuko AI is designed to be review-first and privacy-conscious. Message retention is intentionally limited, and retained content should only reflect the group and workflow approved by the workspace administrator.

Your responsibilities

Workspace owners must use Nzuko AI only with appropriate consent from the relevant group or community members and must not connect private accounts or groups without approval.

Security

We use access controls, authentication, scoped workspace records, and operational logging to protect the service. No system can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid storing unnecessary sensitive information in recap content.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact RohariGroup LTD at info@roharigroup.com.
