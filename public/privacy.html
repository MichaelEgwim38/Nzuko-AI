Nzuko AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Nzuko AI helps approved communities and teams turn authorised WhatsApp, Telegram and imported conversations into reports, action items and voice-note summaries. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

What we collect

  • Account data such as your email address, display name, and authentication details.
  • Workspace settings such as approved WhatsApp or Telegram group names, language settings, and report preferences.
  • Captured group content that your workspace explicitly approves for recap generation, including text messages and voice-note transcripts.
  • Billing and subscription data required to manage paid access through Stripe.
  • Operational logs used to secure the app, diagnose issues, and verify workspace activity.

How we use data

  • To authenticate users and protect access to each workspace.
  • To generate meeting minutes, recap drafts, and voice-note summaries.
  • To support billing, subscription changes, and customer support.
  • To improve reliability, detect misuse, and audit important workspace actions.

Our role and lawful use

RohariGroup LTD is the controller for account administration, service security, billing and customer support. When a business or organisation uses Nzuko AI to process its authorised conversations, that customer normally decides why the content is processed and RohariGroup LTD normally acts as its processor. The customer must identify and document an appropriate lawful basis. Consent is not always the appropriate basis, particularly in an employment relationship.

Optional AI processing

Whole-conversation AI reconciliation is off unless it has been enabled for the service and separately authorised by the workspace owner. When authorised, selected conversation text, relevant speaker labels, timestamps and voice-note meanings may be sent to OpenAI to produce a draft report. Nzuko AI removes common email addresses, telephone numbers, web links and platform identifiers and limits the amount of material sent. OpenAI API requests are submitted with storage disabled. AI output is never an official record until a user reviews and approves it.

Do not use Nzuko AI for clinical diagnosis, treatment decisions, medication records, safeguarding records or decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Healthcare mode is limited to administrative operations unless a customer has completed its own lawful-basis assessment, special-category condition assessment and data protection impact assessment.

Third-party services

Nzuko AI currently relies on third-party infrastructure providers, which may process limited data on our behalf, including:

  • Supabase for authentication.
  • Stripe for payments and subscription management.
  • Netlify for hosting and backend functions.
  • OpenAI for voice-note transcription and AI-generated recap processing when enabled.
  • WAHA or related WhatsApp bridge services for approved WhatsApp connectivity.
  • Telegram's MTProto service through an isolated connector for QR-authorised Telegram connectivity.

Data retention

Captured source messages are retained for 14 days by default and are then automatically removed. Approved reports, action records, account records, security logs and billing records may have different retention periods according to their operational or legal purpose. Customers may request deletion or return of their data when their service ends, subject to legal retention requirements and backup deletion cycles.

Your responsibilities

Workspace owners must use Nzuko AI only with appropriate consent from the relevant group or community members and must not connect private accounts or groups without approval.

Security

We use access controls, authentication, scoped workspace records, and operational logging to protect the service. No system can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid storing unnecessary sensitive information in recap content.

International processing

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where a restricted transfer applies, RohariGroup LTD will rely on an applicable adequacy regulation or appropriate contractual safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum, together with an appropriate transfer-risk assessment. Information about applicable safeguards is available on request.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, individuals may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict their information, receive a portable copy, and object to certain processing. Requests should first be directed to the organisation that controls the relevant workspace, or to RohariGroup LTD using the address below. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
