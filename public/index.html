Nzuko AI Quick guide

Use this quick guide whenever you need help connecting WhatsApp, choosing a group, or preparing a recap.

Start here

  1. Sign in to Nzuko AI.
  2. Click Start session.
  3. Get the QR code.
  4. Open WhatsApp on your phone and go to Linked Devices.
  5. Scan the QR.

Load your group

  1. Click Load WhatsApp groups.
  2. Choose the group you want to use.
  3. Choose the summary period you want to use.
  4. Get messages for that period.
  5. Generate the recap.
  6. Select voice-note language before generating a recap.

When switching users

  1. Click Disconnect and switch WhatsApp.
  2. Start a new session.
  3. Show the QR again on the dashboard screen.
  4. The next person scans with their own WhatsApp.

Only one WhatsApp account can be connected at a time in the current setup.

Install Nzuko AI

Add Nzuko AI to your phone

Install the workspace for faster access and an app-like experience on Android and iPhone.

Android

  1. Open Nzuko AI in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app when it appears in the top bar.
  3. Accept the install prompt.
  4. Open Nzuko AI from your home screen.

iPhone

  1. Open Nzuko AI in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add to place it on your home screen.

Best results

  1. Sign in after installation if asked.
  2. Keep internet on for WhatsApp sync and billing.
  3. Use the installed app for daily recap work.
