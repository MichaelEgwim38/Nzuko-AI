Nzuko AI Quick guide

Use this quick guide whenever you need help connecting WhatsApp, choosing a group, or preparing a recap.

Start here

  1. Sign in to Nzuko AI.
  2. Click Start session.
  3. Get the QR code.
  4. Open WhatsApp on your phone and go to Linked Devices.
  5. Scan the QR.

Load your group

  1. Click Load WhatsApp groups.
  2. Choose the group you want to use.
  3. Choose the summary period you want to use.
  4. Get messages for that period.
  5. Generate the recap.
  6. Select voice-note language before generating a recap.

When switching users

  1. Click Disconnect and switch WhatsApp.
  2. Start a new session.
  3. Show the QR again on the shared screen.
  4. The next person scans with their own WhatsApp.

Only one WhatsApp account can be connected at a time in the current setup.
