Nzuko AI Quick guide

Connect from a computer or phone, choose an authorised group, then turn a useful message period into a reviewable report.

✨

Want to explore first?Create a safe example draft without connecting an account.

🖥️

Computer

Connect with a QR code

  1. Open Connection and expand WhatsApp or Telegram.
  2. Select Get QR code.
  3. On your phone, open Linked Devices in WhatsApp or Settings → Devices in Telegram.
  4. Choose the option to link a device and scan the QR shown by Nzuko AI.
  5. When the status turns Connected, choose the group you are authorised to use.
📱

Mobile phone

Connect on this phone

  1. Open Connection and expand WhatsApp or Telegram.
  2. Select Connect on this phone instead of trying to scan your own screen.
  3. WhatsApp: enter your number, request a pairing code, then enter it in WhatsApp under Linked Devices → Link with phone number.
  4. Telegram: enter your number, then enter the code Telegram sends you. Enter your Telegram password only if two-step verification is enabled.
  5. When connected, return to Nzuko AI and choose the authorised group.
🪄

Computer or mobile

Create, review and approve

  1. Confirm permission. Tick the permission box for the selected group.
  2. Choose language. Leave voice-note language on Auto detect unless you know it.
  3. Load messages. Select Today, This week, This month or Choose dates. Nzuko AI loads the messages and creates one draft automatically.
  4. Review carefully. Check decisions, actions, owners, deadlines, unresolved points and source material.
  5. Approve when ready. Nothing becomes an official record or is shared until you approve it.

Optional: Enable AI reconciliation only when you are authorised to send the selected material for AI processing. Live capture is also optional and is not required to load message history.

Change or disconnect an account

Open the connected channel and select Manage connection. You can change the selected group or disconnect that WhatsApp or Telegram account.
